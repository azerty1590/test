How to play
  1. Open any website and pick a mode. The visible part of the page becomes the arena.
  2. Hider: you get a blank grey slab. With 2 or 3 hiders, each paints in turn and the seeker must find them all. Drag it somewhere and paint it to match what is behind it. Use the eyedropper to copy colours, the stamp for a few pixel-perfect patches.
  3. Seeker: click where you think the slab is before time or guesses run out. Hidden slabs are real objects: they idle and tilt a little, and their bare edges and shadow give them away.
  4. Chameleon Hunt: the extension hides several slabs with tiny imperfections. Spot them all.

Screenshots are taken only when you start a round and never leave your browser. Shortcut: Alt+Shift+H.
